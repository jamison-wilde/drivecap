Notes
Snooze
Zz on a row snoozes that drive in one click; click again to wake it. Zz in the header snoozes everything and collapses the card to its header. A new level crossing or entering dire breaks through either.
Header tools
Snooze all, idle opacity, settings, minimize. The dot left of the title shows the worst awake severity. Both popups are separate topmost windows anchored under the header, so they never fade with the surface.
Minimized
Expanded lists every configured drive and mount, worst first, healthy ones with a neutral full bar. Minimized keeps the same card but only the rows in warning or dire; snoozed drives, healthy drives and unreachable shares are dropped. The chevron switches between the two.
Settings
Snooze duration and font sit in the gear popup; Change… opens the Windows font picker. The duration set there is what one-click snooze uses.

Reading the bar
The bar is runway, not disk usage: full at the threshold (20 GB), empty at 0. Gaps mark the step levels (15 / 10 / 5), so each crossing is visible as the fill passing a gap.
